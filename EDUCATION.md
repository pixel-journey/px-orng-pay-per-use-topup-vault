# Education

Adaptive free credits are the cheap path. Paid credits already on the orng balance are next. Buying more pay-per-call units spends real WAX from a dapp-held vault.

Each call costs **0.01 WAX** (`1_000_000` at 8 decimals). The planner refuses to top up when free or paid already cover the need, and refuses when the vault cannot pay.

The transfer body is unsigned and `sent: false` until a later shell broadcasts.
